Education

Science explained for those discovering it for the first time

DnA offers a free section for primary school children and teachers, with up-to-date, classroom-ready resources that make science education simpler, more relevant, and more engaging.

Donation

Every subscription helps support new research

Every year, DnA allocates 1% of its revenue to scientific research. Subscribers choose the recipient organization, turning their support for science communication into a tangible contribution to the future of science.

Our Mission

Making science accessible is our mission

DnA brings together expertise in molecular biology and climate change. The project has a clear goal: to make science communication more accessible, understandable, and relevant to everyone without compromising scientific rigor. We believe knowledge should not remain confined to the scientific community, but become a practical tool for understanding the present and shaping the future.
